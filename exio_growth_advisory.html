Illustrative purposes only. The figures shown above are illustrative estimates, not a valuation, an appraisal, or a projection of results. Actual results and performance will vary, and nothing here should be relied on as a prediction of what your business will sell for.

Method: today’s value is held at a score of 20, which represents an owner-dependent business that has not been prepared for sale. The scale runs from 3.0x EBITDA at a score of 20 to 6.0x at a score of 90, with each point of improvement adding roughly 0.043x. Anchors are calibrated to the IBBA / M&A Source Market Pulse (Q1 2026, 203 closed transactions), which reported a median of 4.0x EBITDA for transactions in the $2M to $50M price range. Sponsor-backed benchmarks reporting higher multiples cover larger deals and are deliberately excluded. Industry adjustments are applied on top of that scale and range from +2.0x (SaaS and software) to −1.0x (retail, construction, agriculture), with Professional Services as the baseline; the result is floored at 2.0x. Score improvement is illustrative and set by you using the slider. It is not a forecast, and it is not a commitment as to what any engagement will achieve.
